      AI you stay in control of.

      An AI writing to your customers should never run loose. 2Capacity is built so it can't, and these rules can't be switched off.

      You approve every send

      Agents draft. Nothing reaches a customer until a person approves, edits or dismisses it.

      No invented prices

      Every figure in a draft must come from your unit list. If a number can't be traced, the draft goes to a person instead.

      Hands over when unsure

      Discounts, payments, contracts, complaints or a request for a person go straight to your team with a short summary.

      No money moves without you

      Agents can't send payment links, contracts or quotations, and never confirm a booking or promise a refund.

      Frequently asked questions.

      Short answers about the platform, WhatsApp, availability and pricing. Your demo covers the details for your facility.

      What is 2Capacity?

      2Capacity is AI powered self storage management software. It brings units, leads, quotes, contracts, billing, payments and reporting into one platform, with five AI assistants and an integrated WhatsApp inbox for self storage owners, operators and management companies.

      Does 2Capacity work with WhatsApp?

      Yes. 2Capacity connects through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Cloud API on your own business number, and drafts replies in English or Arabic using your live unit availability and prices.

      Does the AI send messages on its own?

      No. The AI drafts replies and follow-ups, and a person on your team approves every message before it is sent. Anything involving discounts, payments, contracts or complaints is handed to a person.

      Where is 2Capacity available?

      2Capacity serves self storage businesses in Canada, the UAE, and worldwide, with remote onboarding and 24/7 support.

      How is 2Capacity priced?

      Priced per facility, by number of units. Every plan includes the full platform, and we show the exact figure for your facility in your demo.
