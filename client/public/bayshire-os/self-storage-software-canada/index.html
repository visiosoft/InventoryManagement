Questions from Canadian operators.

Short answers. Your demo covers the details for your facility.

Is 2Capacity available in Canada?
Yes. 2Capacity is headquartered in Oakville, Ontario, and serves self storage operators across Canada with remote onboarding and 24/7 support.
What does 2Capacity include?
Unit and inventory management, leads, quotes and contracts, billing and payments, reporting, a tenant portal, and five AI assistants with an integrated WhatsApp inbox. Every plan includes the full platform.
Does the AI send messages on its own?
No. The AI drafts replies and follow-ups, and a person on your team approves every message before it is sent.
How is 2Capacity priced?
Priced per facility, by number of units, with the full platform included. We share the exact figure for your facility in your demo.
