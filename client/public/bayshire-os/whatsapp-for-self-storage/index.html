An AI WhatsApp inbox for self storage.

How it works.

From the first message to a signed contract, every step is linked to the lead and the unit.

1. Inquiry arrives

One shared inbox
  • Every WhatsApp message is linked to a lead record
  • Your team sees the history in one place

2. AI drafts a reply

Accurate, in the right language
  • Drafted in English or Arabic
  • Availability and prices from your live units
  • No unit that is already taken is offered

3. You approve

A person sends every message
  • Approve, edit or dismiss each draft
  • Discounts, payments, contracts and complaints go to a person

4. Lead to contract

No retyping
  • The lead becomes a quote and then a contract
  • Activating the contract creates the payment schedule

Guardrails you control.

Choose follow-up timing, message tone, daily limits and which actions need your approval. Switch any assistant off in one click.

WhatsApp questions.

Short answers. Your demo shows the inbox working with sample data.

Is this the official WhatsApp API?
Yes. 2Capacity uses Meta's official WhatsApp Business Cloud API, on your own business number.
Does the AI reply to customers automatically?
No. The AI drafts each reply and a person on your team approves, edits or dismisses it before anything is sent.
Which languages are supported?
English and Arabic. Replies are drafted in the customer's language.
Where do the prices in replies come from?
From your live unit list and prices in 2Capacity, so a reply never quotes a unit that is already taken.
