Remote E-Signature

Send a secure signing link to the customer via WhatsApp or email. No login or app download required.

  • Mobile-friendly signing page — works on Android and iOS browsers
  • Customer views the full contract PDF before signing
  • Sign by drawing with finger/mouse or typing their name
  • Signature is stamped onto the PDF and stored securely
  • Contract activates automatically upon signature
  • Signing link has a configurable expiry date
  • Page shows contract number, dates, and first invoice amount
  • Signed PDF saved to Google Drive and linked in the system

Billing & Invoice Schedule

Flexible billing for weekly and monthly contracts with automatic schedule generation.

  • Monthly and weekly billing periods supported
  • Monthly rate ÷ 4 = weekly payment amount
  • First invoice includes current period rent + advance rent (covers the last period)
  • Contracts 4 weeks or less: advance labeled as "Security Deposit"
  • Optional first-month discount (percentage)
  • Full payment schedule auto-generated with one click

Automated Payment Reminders

The system runs every 6 hours and automatically sends reminders to customers with upcoming or overdue payments via WhatsApp and/or email — with escalating stages and full admin control.

Stage Default timing Tone Channel
Friendly Reminder 15 days before due Gentle — "your payment is coming up" WhatsApp / Email
Payment Reminder 7 days before due Firm — "please arrange payment soon" WhatsApp / Email
Urgent Reminder 3 days before due Urgent — "immediate action required" WhatsApp / Email
Overdue Notice On due date Hard — "access may be restricted" WhatsApp / Email
Final Vacate Notice 7 days overdue Final — "vacate or pay immediately" WhatsApp / Email
Admin-configurable: Every stage can be renamed, retimed, and reworded. Message templates support placeholders: customer name, amount due, unit number, due date, and days remaining. Reminders stop automatically once payment is recorded. A full log of every reminder sent (date, channel, stage, success/failure) is visible inside each contract.

Part 2
Moving Business
The moving module is fully independent — separate jobs, invoices, quotes, crew, fleet, and reports. The only shared element is the customer database. Storage and moving operations never mix.
📋

Moving Leads

Track prospective moving customers from enquiry to confirmed job.

New→ Contacted→ Quoted→ Won / Lost
  • Move date, pickup and delivery addresses
  • Estimated volume (CBM)
  • Source tracking (call, WhatsApp, web, referral, walk-in)
  • Internal notes timeline
  • Convert to Moving Job with one click
🏗️

Moving Jobs

The central entity — full lifecycle from booking to invoice.

Draft→ Confirmed→ In Progress→ Completed→ Invoiced
  • Job types: Local, Inter-Emirate, International, Office Move, Storage to Home
  • Pickup and delivery addresses with floor and elevator details
  • Scheduled date and time slot, estimated duration
  • Crew and truck assignment
  • Costs tracking (labor, truck, materials, extras)
  • Internal notes timeline
📅

Calendar Schedule

Visual weekly calendar showing all booked jobs at a glance.

  • Week and day navigation
  • Each job shown as a card on its scheduled date with customer name, time slot, and truck
  • Color-coded by status (Confirmed = blue, In Progress = amber, Completed = green)
  • Conflict detection: red indicator if the same truck or crew member is double-booked
  • Click any job to open full detail
📋

Daily Dispatch Sheet

Operational sheet for supervisors and drivers — all jobs for any selected date.

  • Lists every job with assigned crew, truck, addresses, and time slot
  • Printable format / PDF export
  • At-a-glance view of the full day's operations
  • Crew contact numbers included for on-road coordination
📦

Pre-Move Survey & Inventory

Room-by-room inventory taken before the move to ensure accurate quoting and job planning.

  • Add rooms: Living Room, Bedroom, Kitchen, Office, etc.
  • Log items per room: description, quantity, estimated volume (CBM), fragile flag
  • Photo attachments per item
  • Total estimated volume (CBM) calculated automatically
  • Recommended truck type based on volume
  • Surveyor name, date, and notes
  • Linked directly to the moving job
👷
